### 🛒 MACS: Merchant Agent Commerce System

**UAVS Hackathon 2026 · 2nd Place**

[Live Demo](https://merchant-agent-commerce-system.firebaseapp.com/) | [Repository](https://github.com/NhatNguyen3001/uav-merchant-agent-commerce-system-claudemaxforever)

**Business question:** How can retailers sell to AI shopping agents while maintaining control over pricing, margins and risk?

In 16 hours, we co-created a retailer-side platform that verifies AI buyers, understands their needs, recommends products, negotiates and processes orders.

**Tools:** Claude, LangGraph, FastAPI, Vue 3, Firestore, Google Cloud.

### 🏙️ Every Flat, Priced

**Microburbs Hackathon 2026 · UTS Business School**

[Interactive Report](https://halinhtran-2205.github.io/microburbs-fableandastra/report.html) | [Repository](https://github.com/halinhtran-2205/microburbs-fableandastra)

**Business question:** How can buyers, investors and lenders fairly value properties with no sales history?

With Team FableAndAstra, I helped value **181,711 flats** across the City of Sydney, including the 72% with no recorded sale.

- **Results:** Around 70% of flats sold within 10% of our valuation, compared with 40% using suburb medians.
- **Approach:** Combined property, building and suburb sales data with property features using LightGBM.
- **Decision support:** Evidence ratings help users understand valuation reliability and identify properties requiring professional assessment.
- **Deliverables:** Valuation dataset, interactive building lookup, pitch and demo videos.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, LightGBM, Microburbs API.
